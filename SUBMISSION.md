# 4. Five-Minute Walkthrough

## Minute 1 — Introduction & Problem Statement

**What I'm showing:** Borrower Copilot landing page.

**What I'm saying:**

> "Borrowers often don't know whether they can safely afford what a lender may approve. Banks may sanction more than you should actually borrow. Borrower Copilot helps you understand your true borrowing position before talking to a lender."

**Key points:**

- The home page presents the main borrowing questions the app answers.
- The design uses a cream background with Navy, Teal and Coral accents.
- The main message is "Know your number first."
- The app is a rule-based educational planning tool.

---

## Minute 2 — Adaptive Questionnaire

**What I'm showing:** Assessment flow.

**What I'm saying:**

> "The questionnaire adapts based on the borrower's situation. Salaried, self-employed and informal/variable-income borrowers can receive different questions. The goal is to ask questions that actually affect the recommendation."

**Key points:**

- Questions adapt based on income type and loan situation.
- Loan type determines whether secured-loan information is relevant.
- Collateral and co-applicant questions appear when applicable.
- The progress indicator shows the current question and total path.
- No credit check and no lender connection are required.

## Limitations

Borrower Copilot is an educational, rule-based planning tool.

It does not:

- Access real credit-bureau data
- Connect to bank accounts
- Retrieve real lender offers
- Guarantee loan approval
- Replace professional financial advice

Different lenders may use different underwriting policies, rates, fees and eligibility criteria.

The rules and assumptions used by the prototype are documented in `RULES.md`.
